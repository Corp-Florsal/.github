# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

FlorSal Group is committed to a respectful, professional and inclusive environment for contributors, collaborators, clients and learners.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Protect confidential, personal and client information.
- Respect cultural, linguistic and professional differences.
- Give credit for contributions and sources.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation and abusive conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, client records or sensitive business information.
- Deliberate disruption of projects or discussions.
- Misrepresenting results, ownership or authorship.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the organization owner or repository maintainer. Reports will be reviewed proportionally and confidentially when possible.

Corrective actions may include clarification, warning, removal of content, restricted access or removal from the organization.
