# FlorSal Group Enterprise Map

## Purpose

This document defines the official relationship between the company, divisions, programs, products, client solutions and repositories.

## Corporate structure

FlorSal Group is the parent organization.

### Divisions

- FlorSal Academy
- FlorSal Trading
- FlorSal Tech
- FlorSal Consulting
- FlorSal Transport
- FlorSal Leather
- FlorSal Naturals

## Shared corporate repositories

- `Corp-Florsal/.github` — governance and organization profile
- `Corp-Florsal/florsal-brand` — official brand assets and brand standards
- `Corp-Florsal/florsal-digital-ecosystem` — corporate digital ecosystem and integration portal

## Classification rules

1. A division represents a business area.
2. A program belongs to FlorSal Academy.
3. A product belongs to FlorSal Tech when it is a reusable software platform.
4. A client solution is separated from reusable FlorSal products.
5. A repository is a technical implementation, not a business unit by itself.
6. Personal learning and professional portfolio repositories remain under `Jorgejfp` unless explicitly approved for transfer.

## Decision hierarchy

Use this order for all future decisions:

1. FlorSal Group
2. Division
3. Product, program or client solution
4. Project or initiative
5. Repository
6. Application, domain and deployment

## Cost principle

Free and open-source tools are the default. Paid subscriptions require a documented business need, expected benefit and prior approval.
