## FlorSal Trading scope

FlorSal Trading is a business division, not an educational program. Its approved scope includes:

- Real trading execution.
- Trading budget management.
- Profit and loss control.
- Capital and cash-flow tracking.
- Risk limits and position sizing.
- Trading journal and playbooks.
- Funded-account management.
- Strategy testing and performance analytics.
- Market research and operating documentation.

The educational content derived from this work belongs to **FlorSal Academy → Trading Program**.
