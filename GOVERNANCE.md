# Governance

## Purpose

This document defines how FlorSal Group organizes repositories, ownership and decisions across its business sectors.

## Business sectors

- FlorSal Academy
- FlorSal Tech
- FlorSal Consulting
- FlorSal Transport
- FlorSal Leather
- FlorSal Naturals

## Repository ownership

Every repository must have:

- A business sector.
- A product, program or client purpose.
- A maintainer.
- A lifecycle status.
- A data classification.

## Repository types

- Corporate platform.
- Product.
- Educational program.
- Client solution.
- Internal system.
- Shared library.
- Documentation.

## Lifecycle status

- Planned.
- Active.
- Maintenance.
- Archived.

## Decision rights

The organization owner approves:

- Repository transfers and deletion.
- New business-sector repositories.
- Public release of client or internal code.
- Changes involving financial, payroll or personal information.
- Paid subscriptions and infrastructure commitments.

Maintainers may approve routine code, documentation and maintenance changes within their assigned repositories.

## Cost principle

FlorSal Group prioritizes free and open-source tools. Paid services require a documented need, expected benefit, estimated recurring cost and approval by the organization owner.

## Separation rules

- Personal learning projects remain under the personal GitHub account.
- Corporate intellectual property belongs under `Corp-Florsal`.
- Client-specific code remains clearly separated from reusable FlorSal products.
- Production data must never be stored in source repositories.
