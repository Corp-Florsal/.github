## Who we are

**FlorSal Group** is a developing business ecosystem that transforms knowledge, professional experience and technology into structured education, disciplined trading operations, reusable digital products and practical client solutions.

Our operating model combines enterprise governance, documented architecture, financial discipline, continuous learning and responsible use of artificial intelligence.

## Mission

Build sustainable business capabilities through education, disciplined operations, technology and evidence-based decision-making.

## Vision

Develop FlorSal Group into an integrated international ecosystem of professional education, financial operations, digital products and specialized business solutions.

## Operating principles

1. Architecture before implementation.
2. Risk management before growth.
3. Reuse before duplication.
4. Evidence before assumptions.
5. Documentation is part of the product.
6. Security and privacy are designed from the beginning.
7. Free and open-source solutions are evaluated first.
8. Every initiative requires an owner, purpose, priority, lifecycle state and success criterion.
9. Client-specific code remains separate from reusable FlorSal products.
10. Artificial intelligence supports decisions but does not replace accountability.
